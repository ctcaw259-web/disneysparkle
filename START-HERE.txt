DISNEY SPARKLE — REVISION 10

DEPLOY
Upload this ZIP into your existing Cloudflare Pages project. index.html,
_worker.js, sparkle.js and sw.js belong at the root. No build command is needed for Pages. Pocket alerts need a separate Worker.
Existing local preferences and photos are preserved. Refresh after uploading.
Your deployed website has NOT been changed by preparing this package.


NEW IN REVISION 10
Trip-day mode: Plan your trip or Countdown > trip-day settings. Save arrival,
last day (inclusive), time zone and automatic mode. Home switches during those
dates to today's published hours, next three fresh shows, starred waits and your
dining notebook. Choose today's park on Home and save it for that date. The
at-home welcome remains one click away. Dates follow your chosen trip time zone.
The notebook now has structured dining date/time/name/location/status entries.
Existing free-text dining notes also appear for the matching trip day.

DVC tracker: on Countdown and Planner, save a snapshot per contract/Use Year.
Enter REMAINING UNCOMMITTED current-year, banked and borrowed balances; do not
enter annual allotment numbers unless those are also your remaining balances.
Edit a snapshot when bookings or banking change your account. This is manual,
not an official DVC integration. No points move and no automatic annual rollover.
The banking deadline is the last day of the eighth Use-Year month. Expiration
is the day before the next Use Year. Borrowing is performed when reserving and
has no fixed annual deadline; an optional personal decision reminder is provided.
Calendar export has 30- and 7-day advance reminders. Import the ICS yourself.
Rules/eligibility/holding points should be verified in your official DVC account.

Pocket alerts: see PUSH-SETUP.txt. The implementation is included, but the
separate scheduled Worker, D1 schema, VAPID keys and device permission must be
configured before closed-app alerts work. The included push-service.zip is a
separate deployment kit, not another Pages entry point.

FAMILY STATION TOOLS
TV & ambient: arrow navigation with large gold focus rings. Enter activates
buttons/check boxes; Escape closes a dialog or returns home. In text inputs,
Left/Right moves the cursor and Up/Down moves focus. In native dropdowns,
Up/Down chooses an option and Left/Right leaves the control. Embedded Spotify
and YouTube players own their own keyboard handling; controls inside those
cross-origin frames cannot be managed by this app. Test your TV remote.
Ambient mode is opt-in: choose 2, 5 or 10 idle minutes, or Start now. It rotates
local photos, countdown and fresh upcoming showtimes every 20 seconds. Any key
or tap wakes it without activating the underlying button. It will not interrupt
Terrace, Piano, Multiview or open dialogs. Showtimes are for the selected park.

Family voices: enter a person's name, press Record, allow microphone access,
then Stop & save (30-second maximum). Choose the person to play their greeting.
Recordings remain in this browser. Download a greeting to transfer to another
device through Family Welcome's audio upload. HTTPS and MediaRecorder support
are required. This is separate from the existing AI character voice feature.

Family sync: after the D1 setup in CONNECTED-SETUP.txt, enter the station code.
Save the first device's settings. On another device, Load shared settings first.
Shared fields: family name, resort, arrival, favorite rides, alert thresholds,
trip notebook, booking choices and Google Photos shortcut. Station title,
playback settings, recordings and conversations stay local. Automatic checks
run once a minute this session; they pause if local shared fields have changed.
Save conflicts reject stale writes instead of silently overwriting newer work.
Photo upload/download is explicit: up to 20 resized JPEGs, 1600px, <1MB each.
Manage shared photos removes cloud copies only; local copies remain. Trip-day settings, dining entries and DVC snapshots are also shared. One private
family collection per deployment, not separate individual accounts.

RIDE ALERTS & TRENDS
Park wait times > Set wait alert. Saving an alert also favorites the ride.
Only fresh readings from the selected park can trigger. Checks run while the
app is open; use Pocket ride alerts for the separate background service. Optional browser notifications
require permission. Trends collect up to six hours of actual readings on this
device; they need multiple samples and are not a historical-data subscription.

PLANNING WINDOWS
Set your arrival in Family check-in, then open Plan your trip or Countdown.
Choose hotel eligibility and DVC preferences. Dining, Lightning Lane and DVC
windows use Eastern time. Download .ics and import it into your calendar for
15-minute advance reminders. Online check-in is an editable personal reminder,
not a claim that check-in opens 60 days out. Eligibility and booking availability
must be verified with Disney. Other-resort DVC rules depend on your membership.

GOOGLE PHOTOS
Save a shared-album link to open the album. For the ambient slideshow, download
selected photos and bulk-add them in Your memories. Automatic shared-album
import and Google Picker OAuth are not implemented; a link does not grant API
access. Google Photos is not cached offline.

OFFLINE & HEALTH
After one successful online load, the service worker saves the app shell,
bundled character assets and last successful public data responses. Last-known
waits retain provider timestamps and become stale; an offline feed is never
invented or converted to a park closure. Health indicators separately show
waits, showtimes and weather as live/stale/unavailable. Weather becomes available
after the built-in weather lookup. Video, Spotify, AI and cloud sync need a
connection; private family/API responses are never service-worker cached.

EXISTING FEATURES RETAINED
Character portraits, hidden Mickey hunt, tutorial, family personalization,
resort images/music choices, real-video Terrace previews/full shows, Multiview,
piano recording requests, trip notebook, seasonal decorations and TV mode.
YouTube and Spotify playback depends on provider/browser/device restrictions.
TV mode is a full-screen layout, not casting. Use Chrome Cast tab or AirPlay
screen mirroring. No physical TV receiver was tested for this revision.

CODE / VALIDATION
index.html loads ONE browser bundle: sparkle.js. Screen implementations use
explicit named composition stages and one public entry point, replacing runtime
function-overwrite chains. development-source.zip contains reproducible sources,
build script and tests; it is not loaded by the app. To rebuild, unpack it and
run node build.cjs from its directory (Node 20+; SQL tests use Node 22+ SQLite).
Regression checks cover screens, escaping, defaults, calendar edge dates,
fresh/stale/zero-minute waits, alert deduplication, Worker route restrictions,
AI/voice error handling and family SQL authentication/conflicts/photo limits.
Browser checked new controls, keyboard focus and ambient wake behavior.
Real microphone capture, D1 deployment, live push delivery, live AI/voice generation and physical TV
playback still need verification on your configured devices/accounts.
